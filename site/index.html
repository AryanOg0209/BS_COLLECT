Why follow-up slips

Nobody owns the list

Overdue invoices sit in a report. Who calls whom, and in what order, depends on who remembers.

Promises live in someone's head

"I will pay Friday" is said on a call and forgotten by Monday. Most accounting tools list what is overdue; few remember who promised what.

Disputes get chased like debts

An invoice that is genuinely disputed needs a different conversation, not a fourth reminder.

How a week works

You keep your accounting software. CollectOS works from the export you already have: nothing to connect, nothing to migrate.

  1. 1

    Import your usual export

    Drop in the CSV or Excel file. You see what is new, changed and unchanged, and which rows have problems, before anything is saved.

  2. 2

    See who to call, and why

    A ranked work queue where every point of the score is a readable reason, with a suggested next step for each customer.

  3. 3

    Send from your own apps

    One click opens your own WhatsApp or mail with a drafted message in English or Hinglish. You read it, edit it and press send.

  4. 4

    Track promises and report

    Log what was said. Broken promises surface on their own. A weekly snapshot shows the position for the owner.

What it does that a reminder app does not

  • Import with a preview

    New, changed and unchanged, before saving. Invoices that vanish from an export are only closed when you confirm they were paid.

  • Work queue with reasons

    No black box. An escalation ladder says which rung you are on and why.

  • Promise tracking

    Kept X of Y per customer. Payments that arrive after a promise raise a "looks kept" suggestion for you to confirm.

  • Disputes

    Disputed invoices stay out of the chase list but in the totals, with a category, an owner and a resolution.

  • Credit limits and ship check

    Check a customer before dispatch: ship, ship with care, ask for an advance, or hold, with the reasons shown. Nothing is blocked.

  • Statement of account

    Answer "please send the ledger" in one click, with a balance-confirmation letter and an Excel file.

  • UPI id and QR in reminders

    Your own UPI id, no payment gateway and no fees. The money goes straight to your account.

  • Interest and waiver offers

    At the rate you set from your own terms, with a draft that offers to waive it on prompt payment. Wording is for you and your CA to confirm.

  • Cash calendar

    Money that actually has a date, week by week, against your fixed outgoings. Overdue money with no date is never counted.

  • Bank-credit matcher

    Upload a bank statement: credits are matched to customers as suggestions with reasons, including a possible TDS shortfall. It never changes a balance.

  • Customer 360

    Invoices, contacts, promises, disputes and history for one customer on a single page.

  • Morning brief

    A short summary you can paste into the team's WhatsApp group: who to call, promises due, payments recorded.

Optional AI assist (a reply reader, a column helper and a remittance reader) is off by default. It masks phone numbers, emails and ID numbers before anything is sent, shows you what is sent, and never acts on its own.

Questions

Do you message my customers?

No. CollectOS drafts text and opens your own WhatsApp, mail or phone app. You press send.

Where does my data go?

It stays in a database on your computer. The only exception is the optional AI assist, which is off by default; if you switch it on, short pieces of text are masked and sent to an AI service, you see exactly what is sent, and every request is logged.

Do I need to connect it to my accounting software?

No. It reads the CSV or Excel export you already produce. That avoids sync problems and keeps setup to minutes.

Will it get me paid?

We cannot promise that. It helps you follow up consistently and remember promises. What customers do depends on them.

Is the interest and tax wording legal advice?

No. Interest, waiver and tax-related text is a starting point for you to edit. Confirm it with your CA.
